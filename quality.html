Quality

You cannot see heat treatment.

Heat treatment is invisible from the outside. A part that was never properly hardened looks exactly like one that was, right up until it fails in service. Everything below exists so you do not have to take our word for it.

Rockwell hardness

HRC and HRB on every batch, at the points the drawing specifies. The number goes on the report that travels with the parts.

Microhardness & case depth

Vickers traverse on a mounted, polished cross-section to measure effective and total case depth — the only honest way to verify a carburised or nitrided case.

Microstructure

Mounted, polished, etched and examined. Confirms the structure is what the process was supposed to produce, and catches retained austenite, decarburisation and grain growth.

Furnace survey & pyrometry

Temperature uniformity surveys and instrument calibration on schedule. A furnace that reads 30°C low will pass every visual check and fail every part.

The system
